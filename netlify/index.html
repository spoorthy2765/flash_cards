How It Works

From a one-word topic to a complete revision session in three steps.

01

Enter a Topic

Type any syllabus subject — or tap a quick chip like Python, DBMS or Machine Learning — and pick your deck size.

02

AI Synthesises Cards

The backend prompts the model and validates the response against a strict schema, returning clean question-and-answer pairs.

03

Flip, Assess, Revise

Study in 3D, self-assess each card, then generate a targeted revision deck for everything you found difficult.
